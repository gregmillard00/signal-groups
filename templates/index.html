Signal Groups

Powered by inter1

Create three groups of three!

Every clip was read by inter1, a model that detects social signals from face, voice and words together. Play them with sound, then group the three that share a signal.

Score fewer is better
How to play
  • Press play on a clip to watch it. Sound matters - inter1 reads tone and hesitation in the voice as much as the face.
  • Tap three clips you think share a signal, then hit Submit.
  • Unlimited guesses. Your score is how many you get wrong, so fewer is better.
  • Solve a group and you get inter1's actual reasoning for every clip in it.
